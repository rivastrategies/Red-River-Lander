Questions, Answered

Is Red River Rewards free to join? ▼

Yes. Red River Rewards is free to join.

How do I earn points? ▼

Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent on qualifying purchases.

How do rewards work? ▼

Every 100 points unlocks a $5 reward.

Do I get anything for signing up? ▼

Yes. New members receive a 200-point signup bonus, equal to a $10 welcome reward.

Is there a birthday reward? ▼

Yes. Members receive $15 off during their birthday month.

Can I use rewards at every Red River location? ▼

Rewards are available at participating Red River Restaurants.

Can I order ahead through the app? ▼

Yes. The Toast-powered Red River Rewards app lets you place takeout orders ahead of time, pay in advance, earn rewards on qualifying purchases, and simply pick up your meal when it's ready.

Do I need the app to earn rewards? ▼

No. You can join online through Toast, but downloading the app makes ordering, tracking rewards, and redeeming offers much easier.
